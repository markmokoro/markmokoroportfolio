Bridging the Digital Divide
in Rural Education

Millions of rural learners like John are falling behind because they lack internet access, digital tools, and well-trained teachers. Our project provides a low-cost, offline-first learning app that equips students and teachers with engaging lessons, resources, and progress tracking — bridging the gap between rural and urban education. With your support, we can ensure every child, no matter where they are born, has equal access to quality education.

My Role — Data Analyst

As the Data Analyst in the group, I provided the evidence that guided our design decisions. I translated research data — on internet penetration, device availability, and teacher digital gaps — into actionable insights that informed our user stories and pain points. These insights shaped the structure of our wireframes, ensuring features like offline access, lightweight design for low-end devices, and gamified learning were not just ideas, but direct responses to real user needs.
